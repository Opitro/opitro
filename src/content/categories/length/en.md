## How to use it

1. Type a number into the first field.
2. Pick the units in the lists under the fields — the answer appears in the second field straight away.
3. To convert the other way, press the arrow button between the fields: it swaps the two units.

## How the units relate

| | In metric | In feet and miles |
|---|---|---|
| 1 inch | 2.54 cm | 1/12 foot |
| 1 foot | 30.48 cm | 12 inches |
| 1 yard | 91.44 cm | 3 feet |

## Where each unit turns up

- Metres and kilometres — almost the whole world.
- Inches — screen diagonals, pipe and wheel diameters, jeans sizes. They turn up even where everything else is measured in metres.
- Feet and inches — a person's height in the US and the UK, and aircraft altitude all over the world.
- Yards — American football, golf, fabric in the US.
- Miles — roads and speed in the US and the UK.
- Nautical miles — the sea and aviation. Speed in knots is nautical miles per hour.

## Where people slip up

- Height is written as two numbers. 5′11″ is 5 feet 11 inches, not 5.11 feet: 5 × 12 + 11 = 71 inches, or 180.3 cm.
- A nautical mile is longer than an ordinary one: 1852 m against 1609 m.
- Screen inches are the diagonal, not the width. A 55-inch TV is 139.7 cm corner to corner.
- A step is an estimate, not a unit. Here it is taken as 0.762 m; yours may be shorter or longer, so a distance in steps is approximate.

## How the calculator works it out

The calculation runs in your browser: the number is not sent anywhere, and the answer appears at once, with no request to a server. Each unit is first turned into metres, and from metres into the unit you asked for.

The factors are exact by definition, under the international agreement of 1959: an inch is exactly 2.54 cm, a foot 30.48 cm, a yard 91.44 cm, a mile 1609.344 m. A nautical mile is exactly 1852 m. The only estimate here is the length of a step. The answer is rounded to six decimal places.
