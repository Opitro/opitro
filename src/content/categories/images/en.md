A photo is more than a picture. Inside the file sits everything the device wrote into it: the camera model, date and time, the shooting settings, and on a phone the coordinates of the place as well. The tools in this section let you see that with your own eyes and decide what to do with it.

They all work right in the browser: the file is read on your device and never sent anywhere. For photos that rule matters more than anywhere else — the address where you stood when you took it travels with the picture.
