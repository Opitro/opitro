h1: Code Scanners
---

Point the camera and get the contents. Or upload a picture when the code is not in front of you: a box photographed in a shop, an image someone sent you, a boarding pass you saved.

The picture goes nowhere. The camera opens inside the browser, the decoding happens on your own device, and neither the frame nor the result is stored or sent by us. Permission for the camera is asked by the browser itself and is revoked in one click.

The one exception is the “Look it up in the open database” button on the product-code scanners: when you press it, only the item number leaves your device, to fetch the product name. Nothing leaves without that press, and the picture never leaves at all.

Live camera reading does not cover every code type on every phone: on an iPhone only QR works that way, the rest are read from a picture. Wherever that is the case, the page says so before you press anything.
