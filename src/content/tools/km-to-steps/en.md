Type a number into the Kilometers field and the answer appears in the Steps field straight away. Under it there is an "Exact" line and a copy icon. The arrow button between the fields opens the reverse conversion, [Steps to Kilometers](/en/steps-to-km).

The calculator uses a step 0.762 m long. That is an estimate, not a unit of measurement: it is handy for working out in advance how many steps a route or the walk to work will give you.

## Familiar distances in steps

- 500 m — 656 steps
- 3 km — 3937 steps
- 7.62 km — 10,000 steps
- 15 km — 19,685 steps
- half marathon, 21.1 km — 27,687 steps
- marathon, 42.195 km — 55,374 steps

## Correcting for height

A step of 0.762 m suits someone about 184 cm tall. A common estimate puts a step at roughly 41% of height, so shorter people take more steps per kilometre:

- 155 cm — 1560 steps per kilometre
- 160 cm — 1510 steps per kilometre
- 165 cm — 1460 steps per kilometre
- 170 cm — 1420 steps per kilometre
- 175 cm — 1380 steps per kilometre
- 180 cm — 1340 steps per kilometre
- 185 cm — 1310 steps per kilometre
- 190 cm — 1270 steps per kilometre

## Measuring your own step

Walk ten steps at your normal pace, measure the distance and divide by ten. Then divide the length of the route in metres by the length of your step. With a step of 0.68 m a kilometre is 1000 / 0.68 = 1471 steps.

## Running takes fewer steps

A running stride is longer. Jogging takes about a thousand steps per kilometre, so for runs this calculator overestimates.

## How accurate the answer is

The calculation runs in your browser: the number is not sent anywhere, and the answer appears with no request to a server. The step length, 0.762 m, is 2.5 feet, the usual default in pedometers. The arithmetic is exact, but the step itself is an average: yours may differ by 10–15%.
