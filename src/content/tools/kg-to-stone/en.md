Type a number into the Kilograms field and the answer appears in the Stone field straight away. Under it there is an "Exact" line with up to six decimal places and a Copy button. The arrow button between the fields opens the reverse conversion, [Stone to Kilograms](/en/stone-to-kg).

You need stone to tell someone in Britain or Ireland what you weigh, to fill in a British form, or to set up an app that takes weight in stone and pounds.

## Weight in stone and pounds

In Britain weight is given as two numbers: stone, and the pounds on top.

- 50 kg — 7 st 12 lb
- 55 kg — 8 st 9 lb
- 60 kg — 9 st 6 lb
- 65 kg — 10 st 3 lb
- 70 kg — 11 st 0 lb
- 75 kg — 11 st 11 lb
- 80 kg — 12 st 8 lb
- 85 kg — 13 st 5 lb
- 90 kg — 14 st 2 lb
- 100 kg — 15 st 10 lb

## Getting stone and pounds from the answer

The calculator shows stone with a decimal part. The whole part is the stone; multiply the decimal part by 14 to get the pounds. For 80 kg the answer is 12.598: that is 12 stone and 0.598 × 14 = 8 pounds.

## How it is written and said

It is written "12 st 8 lb" or just "12 st 8" and said "twelve stone eight". The word stone does not take a plural: "12 stone", not "12 stones".

## Where stone means nothing

There is no stone in the US; weight there is given in pounds only, so 12 st 8 lb is 176 pounds. For that there is the [Kilograms to Pounds](/en/kg-to-lbs) page.

## A quick estimate in your head

Remember three points: 50 kg is nearly 8 stone, 70 kg is 11 stone, 90 kg is a little over 14. Every 6.35 kg is one more stone.

## How accurate the answer is

The calculation runs in your browser: the number is not sent anywhere, and the answer appears with no request to a server. A stone is 14 pounds and a pound is exactly 0.45359237 kg, so a stone is exactly 6.35029318 kg. The large number in the answer is rounded; the "Exact" line under it shows up to six decimal places.
