The stone is a body-weight unit still very much in everyday use in the UK and Ireland — people there are more likely to say "I weigh 12 stone" than "76 kilograms." Handy when talking about weight with someone British or reading a health article from a UK site.

A stone is exactly 14 pounds, so if you know the weight in pounds, that's another way to get there.
