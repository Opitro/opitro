If your phone or tracker shows a step count and you want to know what that is in kilometers, this is the calculator. Accuracy depends on your actual stride — this uses the 76 cm average that most step-counting apps default to.

If you know your real stride length (say you measured it yourself), your number might come out a little different — but for a rough sense of how far you walked, this gets you close enough.
