Type a number into the Steps field and the answer appears in the Kilometers field straight away. Under it there is an "Exact" line and a copy icon. The arrow button between the fields opens the reverse conversion, [Kilometers to Steps](/en/km-to-steps).

A pedometer or a watch shows steps, but distance is easier to picture in kilometres. The calculator uses a step 0.762 m long, so the answer is an estimate, not a measurement.

## How long it takes

At a normal walk a person takes about 100 steps a minute. 5000 steps is 50 minutes, 10,000 steps is roughly 1 hour 40 minutes.

## Where 10,000 steps came from

The number comes from an advert. In 1965 a pedometer called Manpo-kei, "ten-thousand-step meter", went on sale in Japan. The name stuck as a daily goal, but it was never a medical standard.

## How accurate the answer is

The calculation runs in your browser: the number is not sent anywhere, and the answer appears with no request to a server. The step length, 0.762 m, is 2.5 feet, the usual default in pedometers. The arithmetic is exact, but the step itself is an average: yours may differ by 10–15%.
