In the US, air temperature, body temperature, and oven temperature are almost always given in Fahrenheit — Celsius is what you're likely used to, especially reading a US weather forecast or an appliance manual.

Also useful reading US medical sources — normal body temperature there is listed as 98.6°F, which is confusing without knowing the conversion.
