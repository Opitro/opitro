You need ounces when your scales show grams but the weight has to be written for Americans: a recipe, a product listing, the weight of a parcel or a skein of yarn.

## Over 16 ounces it becomes pounds

In the US a weight of 16 ounces or more is written in pounds and ounces. 500 g is 17.64 ounces, that is 1 pound and 1.6 ounces. A kilogram is 35.27 ounces, or 2 pounds and 3.3 ounces.

## Yarn

American patterns give the weight of a skein in ounces. A 50 g skein is 1.76 ounces, a 100 g skein is 3.53 ounces.

## Gold and silver are counted differently

Precious metals are weighed in troy ounces of 31.1 g. 10 g of gold is 0.32 troy ounces, not 0.35. The calculator on this page uses the ordinary ounce.

## A quick estimate in your head

Remember that 100 g is 3.5 ounces and scale from there. 300 g → 3 × 3.5 = 10.5 ounces. The exact value is 10.58.

## How accurate the answer is

The calculation runs in your browser: the number is not sent anywhere, and the answer appears with no request to a server. An ounce is 1/16 of a pound and a pound is exactly 0.45359237 kg, so an ounce is exactly 28.349523125 g. The large number in the answer is rounded; the "Exact" line under it shows up to six decimal places.
