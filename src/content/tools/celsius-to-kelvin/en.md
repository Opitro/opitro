Kelvin is the scientific temperature scale used in physics and chemistry, where temperature is measured from absolute zero rather than the freezing point of water. Thermodynamics problems in school and university almost always want Kelvin.

Useful for students and anyone working with formulas that need temperature in Kelvin when the given data is in familiar Celsius degrees.
