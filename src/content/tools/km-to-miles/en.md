Kilometers are the standard distance unit almost everywhere, while miles are still used in the US, the UK, and a handful of other countries — especially on road signs and in sports. This converter turns kilometers into miles instantly, with no eyeballed rounding.

Useful when planning a trip to the US or UK and you need to know how many miles a km-based GPS reading actually is, or when reading a foreign article about a marathon, a bike race, or a flight distance.
