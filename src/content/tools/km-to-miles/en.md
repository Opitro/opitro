Type a number into the Kilometers field and the answer appears in the Miles field straight away. Under it there is an "Exact" line with up to six decimal places and a copy icon. The arrow button between the fields opens the reverse conversion, [Miles to Kilometers](/en/miles-to-km).

You need miles when you count in kilometres but the other person, the app or the speedometer counts in miles: the US and the UK, running plans, a car hired there.

## Race distances

- 5 km — 3.1 miles
- 10 km — 6.2 miles
- half marathon, 21.1 km — 13.1 miles
- marathon, 42.195 km — 26.2 miles

## Running pace

Pace per mile is pace per kilometre multiplied by 1.609:

- 4:00 per km — 6:26 per mile
- 5:00 per km — 8:03 per mile
- 6:00 per km — 9:39 per mile
- 7:00 per km — 11:16 per mile

## Speed on the road

Kilometres per hour convert to miles per hour with the same number:

- 50 km/h — 31 mph
- 60 km/h — 37 mph
- 90 km/h — 56 mph
- 100 km/h — 62 mph
- 110 km/h — 68 mph
- 120 km/h — 75 mph
- 130 km/h — 81 mph

## Fuel consumption does not convert this way

Litres per 100 km and miles per gallon work in opposite directions: one divides fuel by distance, the other distance by fuel. To get US miles per gallon, divide 235.2 by the consumption in litres per 100 km. 8 l/100 km is 29.4 miles per gallon.

## A quick estimate in your head

Multiply the kilometres by 6 and drop the last digit. 100 km → 600 → 60 miles; the exact value is 62.1. The pairs 8 km — 5 miles, 13 km — 8 miles, 21 km — 13 miles are closer.

## How accurate the answer is

The calculation runs in your browser: the number is not sent anywhere, and the answer appears with no request to a server. Under the international agreement of 1959 a mile is exactly 1.609344 km, so a kilometre is 0.6213712 miles. The large number in the answer is rounded; the "Exact" line under it shows up to six decimal places.
