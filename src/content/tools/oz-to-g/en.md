The ounce is used in the US for food, packages, and everyday items — from a steakhouse portion size to the weight of a letter at the post office. The gram is the everyday unit almost everywhere else.

Worth knowing: the ounce for food/packages and the ounce for gold/silver are different units (regular vs. troy). This calculator is for the regular one, not troy.
