Type a number into the Ounces field and the answer appears in the Grams field straight away. Under it there is an "Exact" line with up to six decimal places and a Copy button. The arrow button between the fields opens the reverse conversion, [Grams to Ounces](/en/g-to-oz).

Ounces are printed on American packaging, used in recipes from the US and listed on steakhouse menus. The conversions for each case are below.

## American packaging

- 1 oz — 28 g, one serving of nuts or cheese on a label
- 4 oz — 113 g
- 8 oz — 227 g
- 12 oz — 340 g, a standard bag of coffee
- 15 oz — 425 g, a can of beans
- 16 oz — 454 g, one pound
- 28 oz — 794 g, a large can of tomatoes
- 32 oz — 907 g

## Recipes: weight or volume

In American recipes oz is weight and fl oz is volume. "8 oz flour" goes on the scales, "8 fl oz milk" goes in a measuring jug, and those are different amounts. For volume there is the [Fluid Ounces to Milliliters](/en/floz-to-ml) page.

## Steaks and burgers

Menus give the raw weight of the meat: 8 oz is 227 g, 12 oz is 340 g, 16 oz is 454 g. A "quarter pounder" is a quarter of a pound, 4 ounces, 113 g.

## Pounds and ounces together

"1 lb 4 oz" is 16 + 4 = 20 ounces, or 567 g.

## A quick estimate in your head

Multiply the ounces by 30 and take off a twentieth. 8 ounces → 240 → minus 12 → 228 g. The exact value is 226.8.

## How accurate the answer is

The calculation runs in your browser: the number is not sent anywhere, and the answer appears with no request to a server. An ounce is 1/16 of a pound and a pound is exactly 0.45359237 kg, so an ounce is exactly 28.349523125 g. The large number in the answer is rounded; the "Exact" line under it shows up to six decimal places.
