Miles are the standard distance unit in the US and UK, while kilometers are used almost everywhere else. This converter is handy when reading a US travel guide, an article about a car's range, a race distance, or a mile-marker road sign, and kilometers are what you actually think in.

Especially useful when planning a US road trip — GPS directions or a guidebook may show distance in miles, and eyeballing the kilometer equivalent without an exact factor is hard to get right.
