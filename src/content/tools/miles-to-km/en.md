Type a number into the Miles field and the answer appears in the Kilometers field straight away. Under it there is an "Exact" line with up to six decimal places and a copy icon. The arrow button between the fields opens the reverse conversion, [Kilometers to Miles](/en/km-to-miles).

Miles are on road signs and speedometers in the US and the UK, in adverts for cars from there and in the names of races.

## Speed limits

- 25 mph — 40 km/h, residential streets in the US
- 30 mph — 48 km/h, towns in the UK
- 55 mph — 89 km/h
- 65 mph — 105 km/h
- 70 mph — 113 km/h, a British motorway
- 75 mph — 121 km/h

## Car mileage

A car from the US or the UK has an odometer in miles:

- 50,000 miles — 80,467 km
- 100,000 miles — 160,934 km
- 150,000 miles — 241,402 km

## Running

A mile is 1609 m: four laps of a track and nine metres more. A 5-mile race is 8.05 km, a 10-mile race is 16.09 km.

## Which mile is meant

This page uses the ordinary statute mile, 1609.344 m. The nautical mile is longer, 1852 m, and is used at sea and in aviation; you will find it in the [length calculator](/en/tools/length).

## A quick estimate in your head

Multiply the miles by 8 and divide by 5. 50 miles → 400 → 80 km. The exact value is 80.5.

## How accurate the answer is

The calculation runs in your browser: the number is not sent anywhere, and the answer appears with no request to a server. Under the international agreement of 1959 a mile is exactly 1.609344 km: that is 5280 feet of 0.3048 m each. The large number in the answer is rounded; the "Exact" line under it shows up to six decimal places.
