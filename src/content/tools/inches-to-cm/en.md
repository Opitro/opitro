Type a number into the Inches field and the answer appears in the Centimeters field straight away. You can enter inches as a fraction, the way they are marked on tools: 3/8 or 1 1/2. Under the answer there is an "Exact" line and a copy icon. The arrow button between the fields opens the reverse conversion, [Centimeters to Inches](/en/cm-to-inches).

Inches are used for screen diagonals, wheel diameters, fasteners and pipes, and in the US for clothing as well. The conversions for each case are below.

## Screen diagonals

- 6.1″ — 15.5 cm, phone
- 13.3″ — 33.8 cm, laptop
- 15.6″ — 39.6 cm, laptop
- 24″ — 61.0 cm, monitor
- 27″ — 68.6 cm, monitor
- 32″ — 81.3 cm, monitor or TV
- 55″ — 139.7 cm, TV
- 65″ — 165.1 cm, TV

The diagonal is not the width. A 55-inch TV has a screen 121.8 cm wide and 68.5 cm high, and it is the width that has to fit the alcove.

## Wheels

Bicycle wheels: 26″ is 66 cm, 27.5″ is 69.9 cm, 29″ is 73.7 cm. Car rims: R16 is 40.6 cm, R17 is 43.2 cm.

## Fasteners and tools: fractions of an inch

- 1/4″ — 6.35 mm
- 3/8″ — 9.5 mm
- 1/2″ — 12.7 mm
- 3/4″ — 19.05 mm

## Pipes: an inch that is not 2.54 cm

With water pipes the inch is a nominal size, not what a ruler shows. A 1/2-inch steel pipe is 21.3 mm across the outside, a 1-inch pipe is 33.4 mm.

## A quick estimate in your head

Multiply the inches by 10 and divide by 4. 32 inches → 320 → 80 cm. The exact value is 81.3.

## How accurate the answer is

The calculation runs in your browser: the number is not sent anywhere, and the answer appears with no request to a server. Under the international agreement of 1959 an inch is exactly 2.54 cm, so the result is exact rather than approximate. The large number in the answer is rounded; the "Exact" line under it shows up to six decimal places.
