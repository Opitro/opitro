The inch remains standard in areas where the centimeter never caught on: US jeans and clothing sizes, screen diagonals, pipe and tool diameters. The centimeter is the base length unit almost everywhere else in the world.

This converter is handy when buying clothing sized in US inches, reading foreign technical specs, or converting a screen diagonal from inches when centimeters are what you're used to.
