Type a number into the Feet field and the answer appears in the Meters field straight away. Height can be entered in feet and inches the way it is written: 5′11″, "5 ft 11 in" or "5 feet 11 inches". Under the answer there is an "Exact" line and a copy icon. The arrow button between the fields opens the reverse conversion, [Meters to Feet](/en/m-to-feet).

Feet are used for a person's height in the US and the UK, for aircraft altitude, for ceiling heights in property listings and for boat lengths.

- 5′4″ — 1.63 m
- 5′8″ — 1.73 m
- 6′0″ — 1.83 m
- 6′4″ — 1.93 m
- 7′0″ — 2.13 m

## Flight altitude

Aviation gives altitude in feet all over the world: 10,000 feet is 3048 m, 35,000 feet is 10,668 m, 40,000 feet is 12,192 m.

## Ceilings and boats

An 8-foot ceiling is 2.44 m, 9 feet is 2.74 m, 10 feet is 3.05 m. A 30-foot boat is 9.14 m long, a 40-foot boat is 12.19 m.

## A quick estimate in your head

Multiply the feet by 3 and divide by 10. 100 feet → 300 → 30 m. The exact value is 30.48.

## How accurate the answer is

The calculation runs in your browser: the number is not sent anywhere, and the answer appears with no request to a server. Under the international agreement of 1959 a foot is exactly 0.3048 m and an inch is 1/12 of a foot, so the result is exact rather than approximate. The large number in the answer is rounded; the "Exact" line under it shows up to six decimal places.
