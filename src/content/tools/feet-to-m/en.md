The foot remains the standard for height and length in the US, the UK, and aviation worldwide (flight altitudes are given in feet everywhere), while the meter is the base unit almost everywhere else.

This converter is handy when reading US construction drawings, real-estate specs, an athlete's height, or an aircraft's cruising altitude — when the source value is in feet and meters are what you're used to.
