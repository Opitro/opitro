## The pounds on top

Weight is almost always given with pounds: "11 stone 4" means 11 stone and 4 pounds. Each extra pound adds 0.45 kg; half a stone is 7 pounds, or 3.2 kg.

- 9 st 10 lb — 61.7 kg
- 11 st 4 lb — 71.7 kg
- 12 st 7 lb — 79.4 kg

## Boxing and horse racing

British boxing weight classes were historically set in stone and pounds: the middleweight limit, 11 st 6 lb, is 160 pounds, or 72.6 kg. In racing in Britain and Ireland the weight a horse carries is announced the same way: 9 st 7 lb is 60.3 kg.

## A quick estimate in your head

Multiply the stone by 6 and add a third of the number of stone. 12 stone → 72 → plus 4 → 76 kg. The exact value is 76.2.

## How accurate the answer is

The calculation runs in your browser: the number is not sent anywhere, and the answer appears with no request to a server. A stone is 14 pounds and a pound is exactly 0.45359237 kg, so a stone is exactly 6.35029318 kg. The large number in the answer is rounded; the "Exact" line under it shows up to six decimal places.
