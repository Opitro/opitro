The cubic meter is a volume unit for large quantities — water and gas meters, room volumes, construction calculations. The liter is the more familiar unit for everyday amounts like a bottle or a pot.

Handy for converting a water meter reading into liters, or working out the volume of a pool, tank, or other container when the source data is in cubic meters.
