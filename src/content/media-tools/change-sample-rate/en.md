faq:
  - question: What sample rate should I pick?
    answer: 44,100 Hz (CD quality) covers almost every normal use case. Lower rates like 8,000-16,000 Hz are for phone/voice-call-style audio; higher rates like 96,000 Hz are for professional audio production.
  - question: Does a higher sample rate mean better sound I can actually hear?
    answer: Not really past 44,100/48,000 Hz for regular listening — the extra headroom in 96,000 Hz mostly matters during audio editing/processing, not final playback.

Pick the target sample rate and hit Process — the converted file downloads as an MP3. Useful for matching a specific requirement (some voice systems need 8,000 Hz), or preparing audio for a particular piece of software or hardware.
