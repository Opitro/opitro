Load a file and its current rate appears in the info line. Pick the value you need, listen to the result with the play button, and download.

## What it's for

- **A system that demands a specific rate** — phone systems and voice bots often accept only 8,000 Hz
- **Matching video** — 48,000 Hz is the editing standard, not 44,100
- **Shrinking a file** — a lower rate makes speech recordings considerably smaller
- **Older hardware or software** — when a program refuses to open a file at an unusual rate

Lowering the rate is audible: the lower you go, the less high frequency survives. On speech it's barely noticeable down to 16,000 Hz; on music the difference is immediate. That's why previewing is here — choose by ear rather than by guesswork.

This tool only changes the sample rate. To change file format use the [audio converter](/en/audio-converter), for loudness use [volume](/en/audio-volume), and for frequency balance use the [equalizer](/en/audio-equalizer).
