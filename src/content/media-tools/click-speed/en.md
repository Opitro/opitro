Click anywhere in the big area — the clock starts with your first press. At the end the page shows how many presses per second you managed.

## What the test shows

- **Clicks per second** — the headline number, presses divided by the length of the run
- **Clicks in total** — how many hits you managed
- **Best second** — the densest single second of the run
- **Best** — your record for this stretch, kept in the browser

The stretch runs from one second to a minute. A single second shows your peak; a minute shows stamina — almost everyone slows down towards the end.

## What to aim for

An ordinary person gets 5–7 presses per second with one finger. Seven to nine is fast, above ten very fast. Anything past twelve is done with techniques rather than a plain press.

A finger on glass gives less than a mouse, and that is normal. The only meaningful comparison is with yourself on one device.

## Worth knowing

We count **presses**, not the browser's clicks. A click only registers after the button is released, and during fast clicking some hits never get there.

Fast clicking wears the button out: in time the microswitch starts firing twice for one press. That is what the [mouse test](/en/mouse-test) checks, and the [chatter check](/en/key-chatter) does the same for a keyboard.
