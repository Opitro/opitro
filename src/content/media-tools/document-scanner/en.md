Shoot a page, drag the four corners, and get a straightened PDF. Everything is worked out in the browser, on your device.

## What the straightening does

A page shot by hand is always a trapezium: the far edge is shorter than the near one and the lines converge. Rotating it does not help. The page recomputes the photo as if you had looked at the sheet from directly above — and out comes a rectangle, as from a real scanner.

## Who it helps

Anyone without a scanner who is asked for a PDF: a contract, a certificate, an application, pages from a book. Several sheets go into one file instead of a pile of photos.
