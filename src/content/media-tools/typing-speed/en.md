Click the text and start typing — the clock starts with your first letter. Correct letters turn green, mistakes turn red, and when the time is up you get your speed, accuracy and mistake count.

## What the three numbers mean

- **Words per minute** — the headline number. A word here is five characters, the usual measure
- **Accuracy** — the share of correct characters among everything you typed
- **Mistakes** — how many characters came out wrong

## What to aim for

Typing with two to four fingers means 30–40 words per minute. Touch typing starts around 60, and 80–100 belongs to people who type for a living. The only meaningful comparison is with yourself: on a phone the result is usually half of what you get on a real keyboard, and that is normal.

Chasing speed at the cost of accuracy makes no sense. Typing at 70 words with 90 % accuracy is slower in practice than 55 words at 99 %: every mistake has to be found and fixed.

## Worth knowing

Nothing is sent anywhere; the result stays on your screen. This test checks you, not the keyboard — if you suspect the keyboard itself, there is a [keyboard test](/en/keyboard-test), a [chatter check](/en/key-chatter) and [how many keys at once](/en/key-rollover) next door.
