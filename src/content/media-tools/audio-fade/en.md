faq:
  - question: What's a typical fade length?
    answer: 1-3 seconds is subtle and works for most tracks. Longer fades (5+ seconds) feel more dramatic and are common for ambient or ending tracks.
  - question: Can I set fade-in and fade-out to different lengths?
    answer: Yes, they're set independently — a quick 1-second fade-in with a longer 5-second fade-out is a common combination.

Set how many seconds each fade should last, hit Process, and the faded version downloads as an MP3. Useful for smoothing out an abrupt track start/end, or preparing a clip for a video project where a hard cut sounds jarring.
