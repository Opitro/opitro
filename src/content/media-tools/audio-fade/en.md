Load a file, start playback, and dial the length in by ear — with the two sliders or a preset. The fade-in is highlighted on the left of the waveform and the fade-out on the right, so you can see exactly which part of the track ramps up and which one drifts into silence.

What it's for:

- **Kill an abrupt start** — when a track opens at full volume and hits you in the ears
- **Smooth out a hard ending** — especially when a file was cut and stops mid-phrase
- **Put music under video** — background eases in and out instead of fighting the narration
- **Blend two tracks** — one fading out into the other fading in, with no click at the join

The fade follows a smooth linear curve, with no stepping or jumps in level. Up to 15 seconds per side, in half-second steps.

This tool only does fade in and fade out. To cut a track use [trim audio](/en/trim-audio), for overall level use [volume](/en/audio-volume), for frequency balance use the [equalizer](/en/audio-equalizer), and to clean a recording up use [improve sound](/en/audio-enhancer). There's also [speed](/en/audio-speed), [pitch](/en/audio-pitch), and [ringtone maker](/en/ringtone).
