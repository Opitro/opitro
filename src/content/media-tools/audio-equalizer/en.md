faq:
  - question: What does each band actually control?
    answer: Bass covers the low rumble (around 100 Hz), mid covers the range where most speech and instrument body sits (around 1 kHz), and treble covers the crisp high end (around 8 kHz).
  - question: Is there a safe starting point if I'm not sure?
    answer: Small adjustments (2-4 dB) go a long way — pushing any band to the extreme (+/-12) tends to sound unnatural unless that's specifically the effect you're after.
