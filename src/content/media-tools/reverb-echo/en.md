    answer: Echo is distinct, repeated copies of the sound; reverb is a wash of tiny overlapping reflections that sounds more like a room's ambience. This tool applies an adjustable echo effect.
  - question: What's a good starting strength?
    answer: Around 20-30% gives a subtle, natural-sounding repeat. Past 60% the echo becomes obvious and dominant.
