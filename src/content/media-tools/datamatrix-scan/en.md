Point the camera at the code or choose a picture — the contents appear as text you can copy.

## What this code is

Data Matrix is a square of small dots, usually the size of a fingernail. It is chosen where there is little room for a code: on a medicine box, on a tiny part, on a circuit board. It still holds noticeably more than a striped barcode.

## What we take out of it

We show the contents in full and as they are. When the code follows the GS1 rules — and traceable goods are marked that way — we also lay it out field by field: item number, batch, dates. Dates are shown in a readable form rather than as six digits in a row.

The product name is not in the code, and we are not going to invent it.
