Press a button and you hear two short bursts — "shh-shh". **Left** plays in the left channel only, **Right** in the right channel only, **Both** in the two together. It plays once and stops on its own; press again to hear it another time.

The check takes half a minute and answers the main question straight away: are the speakers fine, or is it the recording?

## What this tells you

- **Whether the headphones are on the right way round** — if "Left" comes from the right, you have them backwards
- **Whether both channels work** — silence on one side means a break in the cable, the jack or the driver
- **Whether wired speakers are swapped** at the back
- **Where the sound is actually going** — after HDMI, Bluetooth or a dock, the system often switches output on its own

If both sides play evenly here but your music only comes from the left, the problem is in the file, not the hardware.
