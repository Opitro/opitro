Press “Start the check” and the screen fills with a solid colour. A tap changes it; there are nine in all: white, black, three primaries, three secondaries and grey. Look the screen over on each one, the middle especially.

## What to look for

- **A black dot on white** — a dead pixel, it never lights up
- **A coloured dot on black** — a stuck pixel, it always shows the same colour
- **A patch rather than a dot** — not a pixel but backlight bleed or damage

## Before deciding the screen is faulty

Wipe it. On a matte coating a speck of dust looks exactly like a dead pixel, and plenty of people have been caught by that. Look straight on and from arm's length: at an angle the panel shifts colour by itself, laptops especially. On a phone, brightness to maximum and auto-brightness off.

## Worth knowing

The page sends nothing and installs nothing — it simply fills the screen with colour. It works the same for a monitor, a laptop, a phone or a tablet. Next door there are a [keyboard test](/en/keyboard-test) and a [sound test](/en/sound-test) if you are checking the whole device.
