Press “Sweep from the top down” and the tone runs from 120 Hz to 20 and back up again. Going down finds the limit of the speaker; coming back up finds rattles, because a suspicious spot answers a second time and can no longer be mistaken for a stray noise. The large number on screen always shows the frequency playing right now.

## Three things you will hear

- **A steady hum** — the frequency is there, the speaker manages it.
- **Buzz and overtones** — the driver is trying, but the cone wants to travel further than the surround allows.
- **Silence** — below what the speaker can do. The rings keep moving, the signal goes out, and nothing comes back.

The lower limit of your system is the last number at which the tone was still clean.

## The slider is for rattles

A sweep shows the whole picture, but a suspicious spot flashes past in a second. Take the slider, find the frequency where the rattle is loudest and hold it — then walk around and press on the glass, the shelf, the table top. If it goes quiet, the speaker is innocent.

## What this page does not do

It does not measure. The page knows only what it sent: a clean sine at the chosen frequency. What came out of the speaker it cannot hear — that needs a microphone of known sensitivity and a quiet room. Your ear, the volume and the room are as much part of the instrument here as the speaker is.
