Pick a date in the calendar or type one in — the written form appears at once.

## Three styles

- **Fully in words** — “The fourth of September, two thousand twenty-six”, for contracts
- **Mixed** — “04 September 2026”, the everyday form for orders and letters
- **Year only** — the day and month stay as figures, the year is spelled out

## Why documents spell the month out

Written as 04/09/2026, a date means the fourth of September in Britain and the ninth of April in the United States, and nothing in the string says which. Letters end the ambiguity for good.

## Impossible dates are rejected

There is no thirtieth of February, but there is a twenty-ninth in a leap year. Rather than writing those rules out ourselves, we check against a real calendar — it already knows them.
