Paste your text into the field and the time appears at once, in two forms: silent and aloud.

## Two speeds, not one

An adult reads with their eyes about twice as fast as they speak. So a single number would be useless here:

- **Silently** — for an article, an email, a post. Around 240 words per minute
- **Aloud** — for a talk, a voice-over, a script. Around 180 words per minute

## An allowance for Cyrillic

A Russian word is longer on average than an English one, so the same hundred words take more time. The page looks at which letters dominate your text and applies a speed about a quarter lower. Nothing to switch.

## Why this is an estimate, not a stopwatch

Reading speed varies twofold between people. Dense text full of numbers and terms goes noticeably slower than plain prose. The three buttons above exist so you can put in your own speed instead of the average.

For a talk, leave slack: live speech comes with pauses and questions. The usual practice is to add about a tenth to the calculated time.
