Pick a mode, type your text, and the reversed version appears at once.

## Three modes

- **By letters** — the whole string backwards
- **By words** — the words swap places, each still reads normally
- **By lines** — the first line becomes the last

## Emoji do not fall apart

The usual way of reversing cuts a string not by letters but by halves of its internal storage. On plain Latin you would never notice; beyond it the damage starts: an emoji splits into two characters that do not exist, a family-of-three sign disintegrates, and a combining accent drifts onto the neighbouring letter.

Here the cutting follows real letters, so 🎧 stays 🎧.

## Reversing is its own undo

Apply it to the result once more and the original text comes back. That holds for all three modes.
