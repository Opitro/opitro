  - question: How long can I record for?

    answer: Because we deliberately switch off the browser's automatic processing — noise suppression, echo cancellation and automatic gain. Press the red button, allow microphone access and talk. Pause whenever you like and carry on from the same place. A second press on the red button finishes the recording — a waveform appears with a handle at each end: drag them inwards, listen to what is between them, and save.

## What it's good for

- **Getting a thought down quickly** when all you have to hand is a browser
- **Recording an interview or a call** on a laptop with nothing installed
- **A voice message as a file** rather than through a messenger
- **A rough voice-over** for video — record, listen, do it again
- **Checking your microphone** before a call that matters

From there you can work on it without leaving the site: [trim it](/en/trim-audio), [cut the pauses](/en/remove-silence), [clean up noise](/en/denoise-audio), [even out the levels](/en/dynamic-compressor) or [normalize](/en/normalize-audio).
