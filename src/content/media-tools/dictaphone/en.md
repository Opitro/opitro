faq:
  - question: Does the recording get sent anywhere?
    answer: No. Audio from the microphone is handled on your own device and never leaves it, during the recording or after. A file only exists once you press download yourself.
  - question: The browser is asking for microphone access — is that required?
    answer: Yes, there's nothing to record without permission. Access applies to this tab only and ends when you close it. If no prompt appeared, check the microphone icon in the address bar.
  - question: How long can I record for?
    answer: There's no time limit; the counter simply keeps going. Your device's memory sets the practical ceiling — an hour-long recording may be too much for a phone, so split long sessions into parts.
  - question: Why does this sound different from my phone's recorder app?
    answer: Because we deliberately switch off the browser's automatic processing — noise suppression, echo cancellation and automatic gain. They're tuned for phone calls and noticeably damage a recording you intend to edit. If cleaning is needed, it's better done on purpose — [remove noise](/en/denoise-audio) or [even out the levels](/en/dynamic-compressor).
  - question: What format does it save in?
    answer: MP3, WAV or OGG, your choice. WAV is lossless if the recording is heading into an edit; MP3 if you just need to send it.
  - question: Can I record a browser tab or the system audio?
    answer: No, microphone only. Browsers won't let a page capture the computer's own audio without a separate screen-sharing permission — that's a different job.

Press the round button, allow microphone access, and talk. A second press stops the recording — the waveform, a play button and a format choice appear straight away.

What it's good for:

- **Getting a thought down quickly** when all you have to hand is a browser
- **Recording an interview or a call** on a laptop with nothing installed
- **A voice message as a file** rather than through a messenger
- **A rough voice-over** for video — record, listen, do it again
- **Checking your microphone** before a call that matters

From there you can work on it without leaving the site: [trim it](/en/trim-audio), [cut the pauses](/en/remove-silence), [clean up noise](/en/denoise-audio), [even out the levels](/en/dynamic-compressor) or [normalize](/en/normalize-audio).
