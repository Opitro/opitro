faq:
  - question: What's a semitone, roughly?
    answer: It's the smallest step on a piano — 12 semitones is a full octave. Shifting by a couple of semitones is a subtle change; a full octave (12) is dramatic.
  - question: Does this change how long the track plays?
    answer: No — only the pitch changes, the tempo (and total length) stays the same, unlike just speeding up or slowing down playback.

Move the slider to how many semitones up or down you want, hit Process, and the shifted version downloads as an MP3. Useful for transposing a song to match a singer's vocal range, or just for a quick voice-effect experiment.
