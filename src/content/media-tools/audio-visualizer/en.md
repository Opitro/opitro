Choose a track or switch on the microphone, then click a style, a colour circle and a format — the large window shows the result right away. “Listen” plays the sound along with the picture, and the waveform strip under the window seeks through the track. When it looks right — “Download video” at 720p or 1080p, or “Download image”: a frame, the waveform or a spectrogram.

## What it's for

- **Putting a track on YouTube** — the site won't take bare audio, it needs a picture, and a moving waveform looks livelier than a still cover
- **Making a story or a clip** — vertical 9:16 video to the music
- **Promoting a podcast on social media** — a teaser with a running waveform catches the eye better than a screenshot
- **Checking a recording by eye** — the spectrogram shows where it clips or where the highs are cut off

This tool only turns sound into video. To clean the recording up first use [improve sound](/en/audio-enhancer), for frequency balance use the [equalizer](/en/audio-equalizer), and to cut a section use [trim audio](/en/trim-audio).
