Load a track and pick a style — the preview runs by itself and shows you a few seconds of the video to come. Like it? Start the full render and you get an MP4 with the audio inside.

What it's for:

- **Putting a track on YouTube** — the platform won't take bare audio, and a moving waveform looks far more alive than a static cover
- **Making a story or a clip** — a short visualised section set to music
- **Promoting a podcast on social** — a running waveform catches the eye better than a screenshot
- **Checking a recording by eye** — the spectrum shows where it clips or where the top end was cut

Size ranges from 480p to 1080p. Bigger means a longer render — for social, 720p is usually more than enough.

This tool only turns sound into video. To clean the recording up first use [improve sound](/en/audio-enhancer), for frequency balance use the [equalizer](/en/audio-equalizer), and to cut a section use [trim audio](/en/trim-audio).
