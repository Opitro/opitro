faq:
  - question: What does 300% actually do?
    answer: It triples the volume level. Pushed that high, quiet recordings can start to distort — if that happens, back off to somewhere around 150-200%.
  - question: Why is my recording too quiet even after boosting it?
    answer: A raw microphone recording is sometimes quiet across the board because of low input gain — try the volume tool first, and if it still sounds uneven, the normalize tool balances loudness automatically instead of a flat multiplier.

Drag the slider to where you want the level, hit Process, and the adjusted file downloads as an MP3. Useful for a quiet phone recording that needs a boost, or a track that's uncomfortably loud next to everything else in a playlist.
