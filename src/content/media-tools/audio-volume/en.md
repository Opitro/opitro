Load a file, start playback, and move the slider — the volume changes as you go, with nothing to press in between. The value is shown as a percentage and in decibels. There is nothing else on the page, deliberately.

When you need it:

- **A quiet phone or dictaphone recording** — the conversation is barely audible and needs bringing up
- **A track that's too loud** — it hurts to listen to and you want it fixed in the file, not in the player
- **Matching several recordings** — levelling them so loudness doesn't jump between files
- **Background under narration** — pulling music down so it doesn't fight the voice

An honest word about distortion: boosting doesn't create sound out of nothing, it raises what's already there, noise included. And audio has a hard ceiling — run into it and you get crackle. So don't chase the maximum: listen as you go and stop where it still sounds clean.

This tool only handles volume. To even levels out automatically use [normalize](/en/normalize-audio), to clean a recording up use [improve sound](/en/audio-enhancer), for frequency balance use the [equalizer](/en/audio-equalizer), and for smooth starts and endings use [fade in / fade out](/en/audio-fade).
