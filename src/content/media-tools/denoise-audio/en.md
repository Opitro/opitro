faq:
  - question: What kind of noise does this handle well?
    answer: Steady, constant background noise — fan hum, room hiss, mic static. It analyzes the noise pattern and filters it out.
  - question: Will it remove sudden noises too, like a door slam?
    answer: No — it targets continuous background noise, not one-off sounds. A single loud event in the middle of a recording won't be filtered out.

Upload a file and hit Process — the cleaned-up version downloads as an MP3. Useful for a recording made in a noisy room, a phone recording with background hum, or old audio with steady hiss.
