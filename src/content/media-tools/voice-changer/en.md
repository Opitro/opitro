## Fifteen effects, all different

They change a voice in four different ways — pitch, timbre, time and direction — so they never blur into each other.

- **🤖 Robot** — the speech is levelled and multiplied by a steady tone: the living pitch movement goes, machine metal stays
- **🔉 Deep** and **🐿 Squirrel** — down and up in pitch, with the tempo untouched
- **👹 Monster** — far down plus a growling saturation
- **👽 Alien** — up plus an unearthly ring
- **🔁 Reverse** — the whole recording back to front
- **🔊 Echo** — three fading repeats, as if in a tunnel
- **⛪ Hall** — a long tail of reflections, a big empty room
- **📞 Phone** — a narrow 400–3000 Hz band, a horn at 1.8 kHz and heavy compression: the tinny handset
- **📻 Radio** — the whole broadcast: band limits, overdrive, background hiss and a slow wobble, as if the station drifts
- **📢 Megaphone** — a harsh midrange and rough overdrive, an announcement through a bullhorn
- **🌊 Water** — muffled and slowly swaying
- **👥 Chorus** — two detuned copies, one voice sounds like several
- **👾 Retro** — coarse digitisation, the sound of an early game console
- **🎭 Cartoon** — high and exaggerated

## Worth knowing

The slider under the tiles sets how strong the chosen effect is. Everything is computed in your browser: neither the microphone recording nor the uploaded file is sent anywhere. Download the result as WAV, MP3 or OGG.
