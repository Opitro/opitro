Load a voice recording and tap an effect card — the result plays straight away. The intensity slider controls how pronounced it is, from a light touch to full caricature. The Original / Result switch under the player compares before and after.

What's here:

- **🤖 Robot** — ring modulation flattens the natural pitch of speech into a machine monotone
- **📞 Telephone** — a narrow 300-3400 Hz band, exactly what a real phone line passes
- **📻 Radio** — band-limited with a touch of drive
- **📢 Megaphone** — heavy drive and a hard midrange peak, like a shouted announcement
- **👾 Retro digital** — bit-depth crushing, the sound of an early digital toy
- **🎙 Deep voice** — pitched down with a low-end lift
- **🐿 High voice** — pitched up with added presence
- **🎭 Cartoon** — higher still, cartoon-character territory

On honesty: swapping one voice for another — making a male voice convincingly female, say — isn't possible here. That needs neural networks and a server, and we compute everything in the browser and send nothing anywhere. Promising it would be a lie. Everything listed above genuinely works.

To change the key of a song rather than the character of a voice, use [change pitch](/en/audio-pitch). To clean up noise, use [improve sound](/en/audio-enhancer). To shape frequencies by hand, use the [equalizer](/en/audio-equalizer).
