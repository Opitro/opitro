faq:
  - question: How is this different from just raising the volume?
    answer: Volume just multiplies everything by the same amount, so a track with both quiet and loud sections stays uneven. Normalizing analyzes the whole file and evens it out to a broadcast-standard loudness level.
  - question: When would I actually need this?
    answer: Common case — several clips recorded at different volumes that need to sound consistent back to back, or a recording where quiet talking sections and loud parts are jarring to listen to.

Just upload the file and hit Process — there's nothing to configure, the tool applies the same loudness standard broadcasters and streaming platforms use. Handy before combining multiple recordings, or cleaning up a voice memo that swings between too-quiet and too-loud.
