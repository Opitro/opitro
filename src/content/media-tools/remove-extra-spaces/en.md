Paste your text into the field and press the cleanup you need. Below the field runs a comparison row: how many characters there were, how many are left and how much clutter went. Take the result with “copy the result”.

## Four cleanups

- **Remove double spaces** — runs of spaces and tabs between words shrink to one
- **Trim line indents** — spaces at the start and end of every line go
- **Remove blank lines** — several empty lines in a row become one
- **Join into one line** — the whole text becomes a single continuous paragraph

## Where the clutter comes from

Two spaces after a full stop are a habit from the typewriter era. Every letter there had the same width, so the gap had to be widened by hand. Modern fonts do that themselves, and the extra space leaves visible holes in the line.

Indents at the start of lines are the trace of copying from documents, spreadsheets and web pages. You cannot see them, but they get in the way: text refuses to align, and form fields count them as characters.

Blank lines in batches usually appear after moving text out of an editor where paragraphs were separated by two breaks instead of one.

## The order that almost always works

First “trim line indents”, then “remove double spaces”, then “remove blank lines”. After that the text is clean while its structure — paragraphs and breaks — is intact.

“Join into one line” stands apart: it is for pasting text into a field that rejects line breaks. Such text reads worse, which is why undo is always next to it.

## Everything stays with you

The text never leaves your device. No upload, no records — close the tab and nothing of it remains.
