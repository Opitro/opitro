Drop in a PDF and get the text. The reading happens right in the browser, on your device.

## A scan or an ordinary file — it reads both

Inside a PDF there is either real text or nothing but pictures, and from the outside it is the same file. When the text is there it is simply taken from inside: instant and exact. When there are no letters, the page draws the sheet and reads it with the recogniser. You do not have to work out which one you got — the page decides for itself.

## Who it helps

Anyone who would otherwise retype the text by hand: a contract, a statement, a handbook, an article, a scan from work. The gain is biggest when there are many pages.
