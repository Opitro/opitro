Tap the text and start typing — the clock starts with your first letter. Correct letters turn green, mistakes turn red, and after thirty seconds the result appears right there in the same card.

## What the test shows

- **Words per minute** — the headline number, where a word counts as five characters
- **Characters per minute** — the same thing counted in keystrokes, spaces included
- **Accuracy** — the share of correct characters among everything you typed
- **Mistakes** — how many characters came out wrong

## What to aim for

One-finger typing on a phone is roughly 20–30 words per minute. With two thumbs a confident person gets 30–45, and above 50 only a few people go. The same people type twice as fast on a real keyboard — the phone honestly loses here, and there is nothing to be upset about.

The only meaningful comparison is with yourself on one device. A result on an iPhone and on an Android with a different keyboard are already two different measurements.

## Worth knowing

Autocorrect and suggestions get in the way: they finish words and add capitals, and the number stops being yours. Turn them off in your keyboard settings for the test.

Nothing is sent anywhere; the result stays on your screen. If you are at a computer right now, the [regular typing test](/en/typing-speed) is the fairer one — longer text, levels and a breakdown by key. The keyboard itself is checked by the [keyboard test](/en/keyboard-test) and the [chatter check](/en/key-chatter).
