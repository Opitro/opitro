faq:
  - question: The microphone hears nothing — what should I check?
    answer: "Start with the device: when there are several microphones, the system often picks the wrong one — the camera's built-in mic instead of your headset. The buttons above the waveform switch between them. Then check that recording volume is not turned down in system settings, and that no call or app has taken the microphone — many hold onto it even when minimised."
  - question: The waveform moves but the recording is empty — why?
    answer: The microphone hears you, but something is blocking the recording — usually the browser or a device already in use. Reload the page and record again without a video call running alongside.
  - question: Is the recording uploaded anywhere?
    answer: No. Everything stays in the browser on your device, nothing is uploaded, and it is gone as soon as you close the tab.
  - question: Do I have to grant access every time?
    answer: The browser asks once per site and remembers. If you pressed "block" by accident, you can restore access in the site settings — the padlock icon left of the address.

Press "Test microphone" and start talking. The waveform follows your voice — that means it hears you. Record five seconds to hear yourself back.

Several microphones? Pick the right one with the buttons above the waveform.
