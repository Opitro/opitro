## Honestly about ultrasound

True ultrasound starts above 20 kHz, and an ordinary speaker does not produce it. This page gives the upper edge of the audible range — what your device can still pronounce. That is enough for a dog and enough for checking your own hearing, but mosquitoes cannot be driven off by this or any other whine: it has been tested repeatedly and it does not work.

## Careful with the volume

High frequencies are treacherous: there may be no unpleasant sensation while the damage is being done. On top of that, at high volume they can wreck the drivers of cheap earbuds. The volume here sits at twenty per cent for a reason — start there and raise it only if you must.
