Point the camera at the code or choose a picture — the contents appear as text you can copy.

## What this code is

PDF417 is a wide band of small bars stacked in rows. A whole form fits inside, which is why it was chosen for driving licences, boarding passes and access cards.

## What we show

Exactly what is recorded inside, as it is. We do not break the fields down by country: every country and every airline lays them out differently, and a guessed breakdown is worse than honest text.

Neither the picture nor the result goes anywhere. For a document that matters more than anything else.
