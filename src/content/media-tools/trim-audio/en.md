  - question: How do I pick the section?
    answer: Drag the edges of the selection right on the waveform, or type a start and end in seconds — the fields and the waveform are linked, so changing one updates the other. The play button next to the timings plays exactly what you'll download, fades included.
  - question: What does "cut out the selection" do differently?
    answer: By default the selection is what you keep and everything else is discarded. With the checkbox it's the other way round — the selection is removed and the parts before and after are joined together. Handy for taking an ad out of the middle of a podcast, or a fluffed line out of a recording. During playback the marker jumps across the removed region, so you can see exactly what's going.
  - question: What are the fade in and fade out for?
    answer: If your section starts partway through a note or a word, you'll hear a click at the edges. Half a second to a second of fade smooths it over. Both are off by default — turn them on only if you can hear the cut.
  - question: Does trimming hurt the quality?
    answer: The file is decoded and re-encoded, so some of it comes down to the format you pick. WAV keeps everything losslessly; MP3 is written at 192 kbps, which is comfortably above what most source files arrive at. If the clip is heading into an editor next, take the WAV.
  - question: Why is the download button greyed out?
    answer: While the selection still covers the whole file and no fades are set, there's nothing to trim — the output would just be a copy of the input. Move one edge of the selection and the button turns on.
  - question: Can I make a ringtone this way?
    answer: You can, though for an iPhone the [dedicated ringtone tool](/en/ringtone) is easier — it hands back an M4R, the format iOS actually wants, and trims to the length limit for you.
  - question: What's the largest file I can load?
    answer: 1 GB. Files under 150 MB process quickly on any device; larger ones depend on how much memory your phone or computer has.

Load a file, drag the edges of the selection to where you want them, and press the green button next to the timings — you'll hear precisely the section you're about to get. Happy with it? Pick a format and download.

## What it's good for

- **Pulling one verse or chorus** out of a song instead of carrying the whole track around
- **Removing an ad or a dead pause from the middle** — tick "cut out the selection" and the rest joins up
- **Shortening a long recording** down to the part someone actually needs
- **Trimming silence and shuffling** off the start of a voice memo
- **Prepping a clip for editing** — as WAV, losslessly

Trimming changes nothing but the length: volume, pitch and tempo come through untouched. If you need one of those instead — [change the volume](/en/audio-volume), [change the speed](/en/audio-speed), or [fade the whole track in and out](/en/audio-fade). To put several pieces back together into one file, use [merge audio](/en/merge-audio).
