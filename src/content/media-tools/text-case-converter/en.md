Paste your text into the field and pick a style. The change happens in place, and the counters below tally characters, characters without spaces and words on every keystroke. Take the result with “copy the result”.

## Five styles

- **UPPER CASE** — the whole text in capitals
- **lower case** — the whole text in small letters, the usual way to undo all caps
- **Sentence case** — a capital after every full stop, exclamation and question mark
- **Capitalise Each Word** — for headings and titles
- **tOGGLE cASE** — repairs text typed with Caps Lock held down

## What a program cannot do

Tell a proper name from a preposition. To it “to” and “John” are the same word, so “capitalise each word” will write “John Went To The Woods”. Without a dictionary of the language this cannot be solved, and we do not pretend otherwise.

Know an abbreviation from the end of a sentence. “Sentence case” splits on the full stop, so it will capitalise after “e. g.” too. There are usually two or three such places in a text, and fixing them is quicker than going through everything by hand.

Restore abbreviations after lower case. “USA” turns into “usa” for good: the program has no way to know it was an abbreviation rather than an ordinary word.

## About undo

A change wipes the original text, so an undo button is essential here: it remembers the last twenty actions, including pasting and clearing the field. While the tab is open, any of them can be rolled back.

## Everything stays with you

The text never leaves your device. No upload, no stored history — close the tab and nothing of it remains.
